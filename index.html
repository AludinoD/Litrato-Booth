Privacy Policy

Photo Privacy

Litrato does not store your photos anywhere. All of the process stays here within your browser and does not leave your device.

When you take or upload Photos in our site:

  • All Photos are processed entirely within the browser.
  • No images go to any external servers
  • Your photos remain in the browser's temporary memory until you close the website.

Device Processing

Litrato is a client-side website. This means that this does not have a database that is linked anywhere. Everything that happens here, stays here. All the code and processing runs directly on your device, and all image processing happens locally on your hardware.

When you download your finished photo strip, it's generated directly from your browser to your device's storage without any server interaction.
